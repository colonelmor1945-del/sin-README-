# Rockstar outreach

**Status: draft. Not sent.** Nothing here goes out until you pick a recipient
and approve the wording.

---

## Read this before you send anything

Three things worth weighing, because they change what the letter should say.

**Rockstar has no partner programme for fan tools.** There is no inbox for
this. The realistic routes are a press contact, a business development contact
reached through an introduction, or their legal department. Only one of those
three is a route you actively want.

**The commercial angle is the risk.** A free fan wiki and a subscription
product built on the same trademark are treated very differently. The moment a
letter says "we charge for this", it becomes a licensing question, and
licensing questions go to legal. The current platform already carries a
disclaimer, uses no Rockstar artwork, and draws its own map, which is the
correct posture. A letter that draws attention to the commercial layer without
a specific proposal invites a review you did not ask for.

**The ask has to be small enough to say yes to.** "Help us" is not something a
company that size can action. "Add us to the Newswire press list" is. Start
there. A press list place costs them nothing, gets you verified launch data on
day one, and builds a relationship that could carry a bigger ask later.

**Recommendation:** send version A. It asks for one small, free, reversible
thing. Hold version B until you have either a warm introduction or a concrete
proposal with numbers.

## Version A: press list request (recommended)

> **Subject:** Press list request, independent GTA 6 companion project
>
> Hello,
>
> I am writing to ask whether GTA 6 Money Lab could be added to the Rockstar
> Newswire press distribution list.
>
> Money Lab is an independent, fan-built companion tool that helps players plan
> in-game money strategies in Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with or
> endorsed by Rockstar Games, and the site states that prominently. It uses no
> Rockstar artwork, logos, screenshots or map assets; the interface, map and
> illustration are all original work.
>
> The reason I am writing is a data one. The project labels the confidence of
> every figure it publishes: verified, community reported, estimated, or AI
> projection. Nothing carries the verified label unless it can cite an official
> source. Right now that means almost nothing on the platform is verified, and
> the site says so on every screen. Access to official announcements at the same
> time as other outlets would let us label accurate information as accurate,
> rather than leaving players to rely on speculation presented as fact.
>
> If there is a better contact for this, I would be grateful for a pointer.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Baye Mor Mbaye
> Founder, GTA 6 Money Lab
> [contact email]
> [site URL]

**Why it is shaped this way.** It leads with the disclaimer rather than burying
it, so the first thing a legal reviewer sees is that you already know the rules
and follow them. It never mentions revenue. The ask is one line, free, and
reversible. And the data-honesty argument is the genuinely unusual thing about
this project, which makes it the strongest thing to lead with.

## Version B: partnership approach (hold this one)

Only send once you have a warm introduction or something concrete to propose.
A cold partnership letter from an unknown party is filed, not answered.

> **Subject:** Independent GTA 6 companion platform, partnership enquiry
>
> Hello,
>
> I am the founder of GTA 6 Money Lab, an independent companion platform for
> Grand Theft Auto VI players. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, and the
> platform states that clearly throughout.
>
> The platform helps players plan in-game money strategies, comparing missions
> by real hourly return and assets by payback period. Its defining constraint is
> that every figure carries a confidence label and nothing is presented as
> confirmed unless it cites an official source.
>
> I am writing to ask whether Rockstar has any framework for working with
> independent companion tools, whether through data access, a fan content
> policy, or licensing. If the answer is that the current fan-project boundaries
> are the extent of it, that is a useful answer too, and I will make sure we
> stay well inside them.
>
> I am happy to share what the platform does and how it is built with anyone at
> Rockstar who would find that useful.
>
> Baye Mor Mbaye
> Founder, GTA 6 Money Lab
> [contact email]
> [site URL]

Note this version still does not mention subscriptions. If a licensing
conversation starts, that is the point to raise commercial terms, with numbers
and on your initiative, not in a cold email.

## What to fill in before sending

- The recipient address. Their press contact is published on the Rockstar
  Newswire and in the footer of their site. Use the published one.
- A contact email on your own domain, not a personal address.
- The live site URL. Do not send this before the site is deployed and the
  disclaimer is visible on the landing page. It is, in the current build.

## What not to do

- Do not attach the platform's artwork or screenshots of the game.
- Do not describe the project as official, licensed, or in partnership.
- Do not follow up more than once. A second unanswered letter reads as pressure
  and gets forwarded to a different department than the one you want.
